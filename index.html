The living room sits on the second floor with vaulted ceilings and floor-to-ceiling windows, surrounded by trees. Sliders open to a brand new deck that seats 8 — morning coffee, afternoon shade, dinner under the sky.

Walk 5 minutes to a private bay beach with views straight into the sunset. Burton Baker Beach is a 12-minute walk. Wellfleet Harbor, Wicked Oyster, and the National Seashore are all within 10 minutes by car.

We've been vacationing in Wellfleet with our three boys for 8 years. We bought this home in 2024 because Indian Neck is the place we always come back to.
